        FlightSafety Auto-Clicker - Simple User Guide
================================================================================

What does this do?
------------------
This app automatically clicks through your FlightSafety training slides so you
don't have to sit there clicking "Next" every 30 seconds. When a question 
appears, it beeps loudly to alert you, then you answer and it keeps going!


Quick Start (3 Steps!)
----------------------

STEP 1: Run the app
    Windows: Double-click "FlightSafety-AutoClicker.exe"
    Mac:     Double-click "FlightSafety-AutoClicker.app"
    
    If Windows shows a warning:
    → Click "More info"
    → Click "Run anyway"
    (This is normal - the app is safe!)

STEP 2: Login to FlightSafety
    1. Click the "LAUNCH BROWSER" button
    2. Chrome will open automatically
    3. Login with your FlightSafety username/password
    4. Click "LAUNCH" or "VIEW COURSE" to start your training
    5. Wait for the course to open in a new window/tab

STEP 3: Start auto-clicking
    1. Go back to the Auto-Clicker app
    2. Click "START AUTO-CLICKING"
    3. Sit back and relax!


What Happens Next?
------------------
✅ The app will click "Next" every 1-20 seconds (random timing)
✅ When a QUESTION appears, you'll hear LOUD BEEPS
✅ The app pauses automatically - answer the question
✅ Click "Submit" after answering
✅ The app resumes clicking automatically


Controls
--------
STOP button - Stops the auto-clicker
Close browser manually when you're done (don't close during training!)


Tips
----
• Stay nearby - you need to answer questions when they appear
• Keep your volume up so you hear the beeps
• Don't close the Chrome window while it's running
• You can minimize the app and do other things while it runs


Troubleshooting
---------------

Problem: "Python is not installed"
Solution: You're using the wrong file!
    → Use "FlightSafety-AutoClicker.exe" (Windows)
    → Use "FlightSafety-AutoClicker.app" (Mac)
    → NOT the .py files!

Problem: Windows security warning
Solution: Click "More info" → "Run anyway"
    → The app is safe, just not digitally signed

Problem: Can't hear beeps
Solution: Turn up your volume!
    → Windows: Check volume mixer in taskbar
    → Mac: Check System Preferences → Sound

Problem: Next button not found
Solution: Wait a few seconds - videos might still be playing
    → The app waits for videos to finish before clicking

Problem: Browser closed accidentally
Solution: Click "LAUNCH BROWSER" again and login


Need Help?
----------
If something isn't working:
1. Close the app completely
2. Close ALL Chrome windows
3. Start over from Step 1


Safety & Privacy
----------------
✅ This app runs 100% on YOUR computer
✅ No data is sent anywhere
✅ No internet connection needed (except for FlightSafety itself)
✅ Your login info stays private
✅ The app only clicks buttons - nothing else
